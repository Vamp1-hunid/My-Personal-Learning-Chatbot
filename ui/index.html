Library

Books the assistant can study from, grouped by subject. Put PDF or EPUB books, Word (.docx) or PowerPoint (.pptx) files, or .txt / .md notes in the books folder, then press Add on the ones you want. Each book's subject - textbooks, novels, the Bible, notes, anything - helps the assistant pick the right books to consult. Subjects are guessed from the title, then from the book's content once it is added; change any guess, or make your own with "+ New subject".

Memory

What the assistant has learned about you. It reads the most relevant facts before each chat reply. Everything is stored only in data\memory.json.

Tip: in Chat mode you can also type "remember that ..." and it is saved straight away.
